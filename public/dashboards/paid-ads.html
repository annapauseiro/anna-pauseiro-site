What I would recommend

  • Restructure or pause "Broad Generic". It takes about 20% of spend and returns about 1% of revenue. Review search terms, add negatives and move to phrase/exact match before spending another dollar there.
  • Re-test, don't just re-route, the freed budget. Brand Search and Retargeting have the best ROAS but mostly harvest demand created elsewhere, so they will not absorb much more. Put the money into Service Keywords and Lookalike tests with a weekly ROAS check.
  • Fix the LinkedIn funnel before scaling it. Lead cost is high, but only a tiny volume of leads is produced; test a lead-form offer and tighter targeting.
  • Keep Email running. It is the cheapest source of customers in the data.

How the numbers were built and checked

Sample weekly rows shaped like ad-platform exports joined to CRM outcomes. CPL = spend / leads. CAC = spend / customers (media cost only). ROAS = revenue / spend, last-click attributed. Checks run on the companion Excel workbook (linked at the top of this page): channel and campaign totals reconcile to the raw data, and no row has more leads than clicks or more customers than leads. Last-click attribution favours Brand and Retargeting, so their ROAS is a ceiling.
